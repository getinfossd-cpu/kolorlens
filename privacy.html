Privacy Policy

Last Updated: December 12, 2025

1. Our Core Privacy Commitment

At kolorlens, your privacy is the foundational principle of our service. Because our color picking tool operates entirely on your device (client-side), we have an extremely simple privacy policy: We do not collect, store, or transmit any **personal data** or image data.

2. Information We Collect and DO NOT Collect

When you use the kolorlens color picker:

  • **Personal Data (NOT Collected):** We do not ask for, collect, or store your name, email address, or specific IP address.
  • **Image/Color Data (NOT Collected):** The images and color codes you pick are processed only in your browser's memory and are never stored or sent to our servers.
  • **Usage Data (COLLECTED):** We use **Google Analytics (GA4)** to collect standard, non-personally identifiable metrics (like page views, session length, and device type) solely to analyze website traffic and performance for future monetization.

3. Third-Party Services (Google Analytics)

kolorlens uses **Google Analytics (GA4)** to track anonymous website performance data. Our service is otherwise self-contained within your browser.

4. Cookies and Local Storage

kolrlens does not use cookies for tracking or storing your preferences, though Google Analytics may use temporary cookies for its measurement purposes. The only local storage used, if any, is for temporary, functional purposes necessary for the application's operation, which is deleted when the session ends.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page. Since we collect very little data, changes will primarily concern the description of our client-side operations or any future third-party integrations.
